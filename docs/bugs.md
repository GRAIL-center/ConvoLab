## Fixed

| F20 | 2026-08-21 | Partner replies were long and all the same length (measured on 97 real pilot turns: median 53 words, half inside a narrow 40-66 word band). Caused by prompt directives, not the model: "A strong response should usually do four things" forced every turn through the same four beats, and "3-6 sentences / 6-8 when challenged" appeared TWICE in each populist persona alongside "prioritize argument quality over strict brevity" | Partner monologued at participants and read as scripted; a wall of text on turn one risks disengagement before the conversation starts | Single shared `withResponseLength()` policy on all four study personas: most replies 1-3 sentences, 4 max, explicit instruction to vary length. Four-part recipe demoted from mandate to a pick-one-or-two menu. Duplicated and length-inflating rules deleted. First-reply brevity added to `buildStudyPrompt`, i.e. at the END of the assembled prompt, the position where the existing "Begin with..." instruction demonstrably fires |
| B9 | 2026-08-21 | The left and right partner personas differ hugely in size: progressive ~7.4-7.7k chars, populist ~16-17.8k chars — the right-wing personas are ~2.2x more detailed. Partner ideology is a randomised factor | Persona richness is confounded with ideology: differences in how consistently or vividly the partner behaves cannot be separated from the ideology manipulation itself | Open. Should be equalised as part of the persona-fidelity work Nebras raised |
| F18 | 2026-08-18 | The pilot landing page told treatment participants "Open the Coach tab anytime". There is no tab: the coach is a right-hand sidebar that does not appear until the participant starts typing | Treatment-arm participants were instructed to find a control that does not exist, in the arm where the coach IS the treatment | Copy rewritten to describe what they actually see. | F19 | 2026-08-18 | Production pilot ran code on no branch (was B5), and `main`'s `study.ts` lacked the returning-participant shim fix (was B7) | Pilot unreproducible; a rebuild from `main` would have shipped a regression | Sarrah pushed `pilot-qualtrics-flow`, merged as PR #94. | F16 | 2026-08-18 | Study conversation window was too short: 7-min soft cap / 8-min hard stop, and the survey unlocked only on a 6-turn count, so a slower participant could be auto-redirected before finishing a real exchange | Conversations were cut off before participants had said what they wanted; the "wrapping up soon" banner fired at 5:30 | Soft cap 8 min, hard stop 12 min. | F14 | 2026-08-18 | Participant's own message rendered twice: the optimistic echo uses `id: Date.now()` (a number) while the server id is a Firestore string, so they can never match. `score:update` normally rewrites the optimistic id, but if a reconnect replays history first it bailed with `return prev` and left the twin on screen | Visible transcript corruption in the pilot — the participant sees their message duplicated, one copy carrying the provisional tone border and one unscored | Optimistic messages now carry `isOptimistic`; the history merge dedupes them against server copies by content with a count-aware multiset (so a genuinely repeated turn survives), and `score:update` drops the stale twin instead of bailing |
| F15 | 2026-08-18 | The two populist personas never stated their gender in the second person — only third-person "She believes..." — so the model fell back on masculine-default idiom. Live pilot: Megan Briggs said "Guys like me". ## Process

- Log every bug here when found (one row: date, symptom, impact), even if fixed
  in the same commit — the log is the searchable history.
- Fix as we go: small, verified fixes commit directly to `main`; anything
  risky or behavior-changing gets flagged to the team first.
