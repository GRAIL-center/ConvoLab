## Fixed

| F20 | 2026-08-21 | Partner replies were long and all the same length (measured on 97 real pilot turns: median 53 words, half inside a narrow 40-66 word band). Caused by prompt directives, not the model: "A strong response should usually do four things" forced every turn through the same four beats, and "3-6 sentences / 6-8 when challenged" appeared TWICE in each populist persona alongside "prioritize argument quality over strict brevity" | Partner monologued at participants and read as scripted; a wall of text on turn one risks disengagement before the conversation starts | Single shared `withResponseLength()` policy on all four study personas: most replies 1-3 sentences, 4 max, explicit instruction to vary length. Four-part recipe demoted from mandate to a pick-one-or-two menu. Duplicated and length-inflating rules deleted. First-reply brevity added to `buildStudyPrompt`, i.e. at the END of the assembled prompt, the position where the existing "Begin with..." instruction demonstrably fires |
| B9 | 2026-08-21 | The left and right partner personas differ hugely in size: progressive ~7.4-7.7k chars, populist ~16-17.8k chars — the right-wing personas are ~2.2x more detailed. Partner ideology is a randomised factor | Persona richness is confounded with ideology: differences in how consistently or vividly the partner behaves cannot be separated from the ideology manipulation itself | Open. ## Process

- Log every bug here when found (one row: date, symptom, impact), even if fixed
  in the same commit — the log is the searchable history.
- Fix as we go: small, verified fixes commit directly to `main`; anything
  risky or behavior-changing gets flagged to the team first.
